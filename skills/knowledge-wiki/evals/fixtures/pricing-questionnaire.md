**Source:** local file, pricing questionnaire
**Retrieved:** 2026-09-01
**Formatting convention:** questions are `###` headings. Answers are blockquotes labeled **Answer**. Everything else is framing text.

---

### Are prices fixed at the moment a case is submitted, or at invoicing?

_Why this matters: a price change between submission and invoice changes what the client is charged._

> **Answer** — Lab owner: The price is fixed at submission. Whatever the price list said the day the doctor submitted is what goes on the invoice.

### Can a Paid invoice be corrected?

> **Answer** — Lab owner: A Paid invoice cannot be edited. If something is wrong you issue a credit note and a new invoice.

### Do all clients pay the same price for the same service?

> **Answer** — Lab owner: No. Every client sits in a price group, and a client can also have a customer-specific price that overrides the group price for one service.
