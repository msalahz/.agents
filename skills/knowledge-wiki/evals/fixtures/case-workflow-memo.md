# Case workflow memo

Written by the lab owner for new front-desk staff. Read it before taking your first case.

## Intake

A case starts when the doctor's prescription and the impression arrive together. Front desk logs the case the same day, even if the impression is damaged. A damaged impression is logged and then flagged for a remake request; it is never silently discarded.

Every case gets a case number at intake. The case number never changes, even when the case is remade.

## Turnaround

Turnaround is counted in working days from the day after intake.

| Case type | Standard turnaround | Rush turnaround |
|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Crown     | 7 working days     | 3 working days  |
| Bridge    | 10 working days    | 5 working days  |
| Denture   | 12 working days    | not offered     |

Rush is a request, not a promise. The lab confirms rush by phone before the case is accepted as rush.

## Remakes

A remake keeps the original case number and gets a suffix, so the first remake of case 4512 is 4512-R1. The lab pays for a remake when the fault is ours. The doctor pays when the impression or the prescription was the cause.

Three things are never remade for free:

- a case where the doctor changed the prescription after intake
- a case returned more than 90 days after delivery
- a case where the patient refused the fitting

## Delivery

Cases go out with the courier that leaves at 15:00. A case finished after 15:00 goes out the next working day. The doctor signs the delivery slip; an unsigned slip means the case counts as undelivered.
