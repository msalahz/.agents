# Billing questionnaire

Answers from the billing lead, collected over two calls in September. Read the whole thing before asking again.

How is an invoice numbered?

**Priya: Year, dash, then a five digit counter that resets every January. 2026-00001 was the first one this year.**

When does an invoice become overdue?

**Priya: Thirty days after the issue date. We do not count the issue date itself.**

What happens to an overdue invoice after ninety days?

Can a paid invoice be edited?

**Priya: No. We issue a credit note and a new invoice. The old number stays in the ledger.**

That is everything from the second call. The refund questions are still open.
